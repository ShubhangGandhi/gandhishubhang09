About Me

I'm a third-year Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering, Pune — deeply passionate about systems that merge hardware precision with intelligent software to solve real human problems.

My work spans embedded firmware design, IoT platforms, edge-AI deployment on constrained hardware, PCB layout with Altium Designer, and real-time signal processing. I don't just build prototypes — I engineer systems with proper protection circuits, calibrated sensors, and clean, interrupt-driven firmware architecture ready for the real world.

I've registered two copyrights with the Government of India for original engineering products, qualified for SPPU Avishkar (state-level research), and actively compete at national technical events. I speak fluent English, Hindi, Marathi, and Gujarati — making collaboration across India effortless.
